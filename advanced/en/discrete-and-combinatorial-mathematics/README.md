# Discrete and Combinatorial Mathematics (离散与组合数学) — Advanced Level

Beyond the introductory counting toolkit, the Advanced Level adds structured
counting over multisets, equivalence classes, and three powerhouse tools: the
pigeonhole principle, the binomial theorem, and inclusion–exclusion.

## Syllabus items

* 【6】Multisets (多重集合)
* 【6】Equivalence relations and equivalence classes (等价关系与等价类)
* 【6】Permutations of multisets (多重集上的排列)
* 【6】Combinations of multisets (多重集上的组合)
* 【6】Derangements, circular permutations (错排列、圆排列)
* 【6】Pigeonhole principle (鸽巢原理)
* 【6】Binomial theorem (二项式定理)
* 【7】Inclusion–exclusion principle (容斥原理)
* 【7】Catalan numbers (卡特兰数)
