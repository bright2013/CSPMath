# Discrete and Combinatorial Mathematics (离散与组合数学)

Combinatorial mathematics is about **counting**: how many ways can something be done,
chosen, or arranged? At the Introductory Level (入门级 / CSP-J) it appears both as
direct counting problems and as the reasoning behind algorithm analysis.

## Syllabus items

* 【2】Sets (集合)
* 【2】Addition principle (加法原理)
* 【2】Multiplication principle (乘法原理)
* 【4】Permutations (排列)
* 【4】Combinations (组合)
* 【4】Pascal's (Yang Hui) triangle (杨辉三角)

## Subsections

1. [Sets](sets.md) — membership, subsets, union/intersection, cardinality.
2. [Addition Principle](addition-principle.md) — counting by mutually exclusive cases.
3. [Multiplication Principle](multiplication-principle.md) — counting step by step.
4. [Permutations](permutations.md) — ordered arrangements, A(n, m).
5. [Combinations](combinations.md) — unordered selections, C(n, m).
6. [Pascal's (Yang Hui) Triangle](pascals-triangle.md) — binomial coefficients and
   the recurrence C(n, m) = C(n−1, m−1) + C(n−1, m).
