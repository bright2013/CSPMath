# Elementary Mathematics (初等数学)

## Syllabus items

* 【1】Algebra (junior high school part) (代数·初中部分)
* 【1】Geometry (junior high school part) (几何·初中部分)

## Algebra 代数

The junior-high algebra assumed by the syllabus includes:

* **Algebraic expressions (代数式)**: monomials, polynomials; addition, subtraction
  and multiplication of polynomials.
* **Equations (方程)**: linear equations in one variable, systems of linear equations,
  quadratic equations (including the quadratic formula and Vieta's relations).
* **Inequalities (不等式)**: linear inequalities in one variable and their solution sets.
* **Functions (函数)**: the concept of a function, linear functions, quadratic
  functions and their graphs; direct and inverse proportion.
* **Exponents and radicals (指数与根式)**: integer exponents, square roots, and the
  laws of exponents.
* **Factorization (因式分解)**: common factor extraction, formula method
  (difference of squares, perfect squares), cross method.

## Geometry 几何

The junior-high geometry assumed by the syllabus includes:

* **Basic figures (基本图形)**: points, lines, rays, segments, angles; intersecting and
  parallel lines.
* **Triangles (三角形)**: congruence and similarity criteria, the Pythagorean theorem,
  properties of isosceles and right triangles.
* **Quadrilaterals (四边形)**: parallelograms, rectangles, rhombi, squares, trapezoids
  and their properties.
* **Circles (圆)**: central and inscribed angles, tangents, arc length and sector area.
* **Measurement (度量)**: perimeter and area of common plane figures, surface area and
  volume of common solids.
* **Coordinate geometry (平面直角坐标系)**: the Cartesian plane, coordinates of points,
  distance between two points.
